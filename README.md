# Car Market Trends Analysis using Car Dekho Data

## Project Overview

This project analyzes the Car Dekho vehicle dataset to identify important trends and patterns in the used-vehicle market. The analysis focuses on vehicle pricing, depreciation, resale value, vehicle age, kilometers driven, fuel type, seller type, transmission, ownership, and differences between cars and two-wheelers.

The project uses Python-based exploratory data analysis and visualization to convert raw vehicle data into meaningful insights.

## Objectives

- Analyze the selling price and present price of vehicles.
- Identify the minimum and maximum selling prices.
- Study vehicle manufacturing years and vehicle age.
- Identify the most frequently occurring vehicle models.
- Analyze fuel type, seller type, transmission, and ownership patterns.
- Calculate vehicle depreciation and depreciation percentage.
- Identify vehicles with relatively high and low depreciation.
- Study the relationship between vehicle age, kilometers driven, and selling price.
- Analyze vehicles manufactured after 2014.
- Perform separate analysis for cars and two-wheelers.
- Identify the oldest, newest, and most frequently occurring vehicles.
- Identify exceptional deals based on price retention.
- Present the findings using data visualizations.

## Dataset

The project uses the **Car Dekho vehicle dataset**.

### Dataset Features

The dataset contains the following columns:

- `Car_Name` – Name of the vehicle
- `Year` – Manufacturing year
- `Selling_Price` – Selling price of the vehicle
- `Present_Price` – Current/present price of the vehicle
- `Kms_Driven` – Kilometers driven
- `Fuel_Type` – Fuel type of the vehicle
- `Seller_Type` – Type of seller
- `Transmission` – Manual or automatic
- `Owner` – Number of previous owners

## Key Insights

- The dataset contains **301 vehicle records**.
- Vehicles were manufactured between **2003 and 2018**.
- There are **98 unique vehicle models**.
- **City** is the most frequently occurring vehicle model.
- The dataset contains **106 vehicles sold by individual sellers**.
- There are **40 automatic transmission vehicles**.
- Depreciation varies considerably across different vehicle models.
- Fuel type shows differences in average depreciation.
- Separate analysis of cars and two-wheelers helps identify differences in their pricing and resale patterns.
